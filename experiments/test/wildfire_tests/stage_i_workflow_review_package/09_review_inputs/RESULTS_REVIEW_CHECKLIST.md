# Results Review Checklist

- All expected rows present.
- Row counts reconciled.
- All method families present.
- Failed jobs visible.
- Solver gap reported.
- Projection success rates and failures visible.
- Duplicate topology visibility.
- Cached projection identity documented.
- Risk and load provenance sums inspectable.
- Common evaluator consistency.
- Rho-dependent versus rho-invariant methods labeled.
- Comparisons do not mix incompatible PAC definitions.
- Pareto curves connect only comparable points.
- Plots match tables.
- Conclusions distinguish DC feasibility, common diagnostics, GridFM PAC, and AC projection distance.
- No claim of global optimality for guided GridFM search.
- No claim of AC feasibility based only on GridFM or DC outputs.
